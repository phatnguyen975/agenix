# Clarification and Decision Review

## Clarify Material Uncertainty

1. Inspect relevant requirements, schema evidence, and prior decisions before asking.
2. State the affected choice, known facts, missing or conflicting evidence, and consequences for data, consumers, or rollout.
3. Ask concise questions about decisions that change the outcome. Present viable options and an evidence-based recommendation where useful. Continue analysis that does not depend on the answer.

Examples include unclear ownership, cardinality, uniqueness scope, retention, compatibility obligations, and contradictory baseline evidence. Record a non-blocking assumption with its basis and what would invalidate it.

## Record Decisions

- For a consequential change, present why it is needed, its smallest viable scope, alternatives, impact, order, and recovery. Record approval, rejection, or an open decision according to the caller's process and actual authority.
- Reuse approvals that still cover the same choice and consumers. If evidence materially changes the decision, request reconsideration only for the affected scope.
- Deliver the supported analysis even when a material decision is open. Mark the affected work blocked and specify what would unblock it.
- A related shared documentation or schema change has its own owner and authorization. Record the required follow-up without editing the baseline as a side effect of analysis.
