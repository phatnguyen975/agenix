# Finalize the Feature

## Inputs and Delivery Set

Resolve the feature through the catalog and refresh the default branch. Pin its integrated revision and read the Git-owned feature record, approved spec and acceptance, applicable contracts, delivery strategy, ticket mappings, test strategy, `environment.agenix.json`, exposure boundary and durable evidence. Verify the selected tracker and applicable scoped instructions. Reconcile an existing finalization branch or PR and its approvals before taking new action.

Apply [Decide Readiness and Record Progress](../../../references/work-items-and-evidence.md#decide-readiness-and-record-progress), [Specify Verification and Assess Evidence](../../../references/work-items-and-evidence.md#specify-verification-and-assess-evidence), applicable scoped [Human Overrides](../../../references/work-items-and-evidence.md#human-overrides), and the configured [Local](../../../references/trackers/local.md#read-verify-and-update), [GitHub](../../../references/trackers/github.md#confirm-state-and-closure) or [Jira](../../../references/trackers/jira.md#confirm-native-state) rules. In local mode use `verify-status.py` with the tracker guide's applicable policy and delivery inputs. In remote modes verify native ticket and task mappings, states and evidence as well as Git and PR facts at their sources; the feature remains Git-owned in every mode.

Verify every non-cancelled ticket is merged and `DONE` with valid completion evidence, and reconcile the nonempty delivery set, approved cancellations and feature acceptance coverage. Apply only an explicitly scoped approved override; keep unmet conditions and actual results visible. Resolve missing or stale evidence before dependent acceptance. Record `IN-REVIEW` for feature acceptance in its active checkpoint.

## Verify Integrated Acceptance

On the pinned integrated revision, rerun the distinct applicable verification commands from delivered tickets and the feature-level checks selected by acceptance and the approved test strategy. Cover required real client/provider integration and end-to-end journeys through the owned public boundary with exposure isolated. Reuse evidence only for the same check on the same candidate and context. Explain historical ticket checks that do not apply to the integrated candidate; do not claim mock-only evidence proves real integration.

Use [Playwright CLI](../../playwright-cli/SKILL.md), [Accessibility](../../accessibility/SKILL.md) or [Web Performance](../../web-perf/SKILL.md) only when an approved applicable check requires their browser, accessibility or measurement method. Use an approved equivalent when it supplies the same evidence. Record commands, targets, results, revision, limitations and actual required human confirmations. Do not claim unrun checks passed.

A coverage gap, failed required check or material contract conflict blocks finalization. Resolve harness failures within authorized scope; route product gaps through approved new tickets. Reassess and rerun affected checks if the ticket set, contracts or integrated baseline change; obtain fresh approval for materially changed candidates.

## Publish and Verify Completion

Prepare prospective `DONE` and embed the acceptance checkpoint in `docs/features/<feature-id>/feature.agenix.json` on `docs/status-<feature-id>` using the [Checkpoint Schema](../../../templates/evidence/checkpoint.schema.json). Record actual results and pending checks without inventing future merge evidence. Use the [Checkpoint Template](../../../templates/evidence/checkpoint.md) for applicable PR comments and keep the Git-owned record authoritative. Apply [Artifact Gates](../../../references/workflow-control.md#artifact-gates): present the pinned baseline, delivery coverage, real-boundary and manual results, remaining PR checks, completion candidate and exact publication actions. After approval, [publish the candidate](../../../references/git-publication.md#publish-the-approved-candidate) and create or update the feature finalization PR. Do not claim completion from prospective `DONE` alone.

Use [Bind Review and CI to the Candidate](../../../references/git-publication.md#bind-review-and-ci-to-the-candidate) to verify acceptance evidence, required native review and CI on the final head. Apply [Merge the Approved Head](../../../references/git-publication.md#merge-the-approved-head), requesting a separate human decision for the exact PR and head. A human merge or queue submission remains pending until actual merge is confirmed. Apply [Verify Delivery and Recover from a Failed Check](../../../references/git-publication.md#verify-delivery-and-recover-from-a-failed-check): verify the merge SHA, approved completion record and required post-merge checks on the refreshed default branch; bind them to durable PR evidence. If a required post-merge check fails, do not claim completion or exposure. Reconcile and report the blocker under the applicable recovery rules.

If an already merged completion record becomes obsolete, use a new scoped PR under the amendment rules; do not rewrite history. Once completion is verified, apply [Authorize Feature Exposure](../../../references/git-publication.md#authorize-feature-exposure) only when requested and separately authorized for the exact operation, target and verified revision or artifact. Deferred exposure follows the project's operational workflow. Report acceptance, actual merge, verified completion and exposure separately. Follow the shared cleanup and session closure rules.
